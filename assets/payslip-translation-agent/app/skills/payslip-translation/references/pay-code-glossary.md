# Pay Code Glossary — Common European Payroll Codes

Reference table of common pay code patterns across major European payroll systems. Use this for accurate plain-language explanations.

## German Payroll Codes (DE)

| Code | German Label | English Meaning | Plain Explanation |
|------|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| LSt KL I | Lohnsteuer Klasse I | Wage Tax Class I | Income tax for single employees without children |
| LSt KL IV | Lohnsteuer Klasse IV | Wage Tax Class IV | Income tax for married couples, both employed |
| KV AG | Krankenversicherung Arbeitgeber | Health Insurance (Employer) | Your employer's share of your statutory health insurance premium |
| KV AN | Krankenversicherung Arbeitnehmer | Health Insurance (Employee) | Your share of the statutory health insurance premium |
| RV AG | Rentenversicherung Arbeitgeber | Pension Insurance (Employer) | Employer contribution to the statutory pension fund |
| RV AN | Rentenversicherung Arbeitnehmer | Pension Insurance (Employee) | Your contribution to the statutory pension fund |
| AV AG | Arbeitslosenversicherung AG | Unemployment Insurance (Employer) | Employer's contribution to unemployment insurance |
| AV AN | Arbeitslosenversicherung AN | Unemployment Insurance (Employee) | Your contribution to unemployment insurance |
| PV AN | Pflegeversicherung Arbeitnehmer | Long-term Care Insurance | Your contribution to statutory long-term care insurance |
| SV Abzug | Sozialversicherungsabzug | Social Security Deduction | Total social security deductions |
| Grundgehalt | Grundgehalt | Base Salary | Your fixed monthly gross salary |
| Urlaubsgeld | Urlaubsgeld | Holiday Pay | Additional pay for your annual leave period |
| Weihnachtsgeld | Weihnachtsgeld | Christmas Bonus | Annual holiday bonus |

## French Payroll Codes (FR)

| Code | French Label | English Meaning | Plain Explanation |
|------|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| SMIC | Salaire Minimum | Minimum Wage Component | Component linked to the statutory minimum wage |
| CSG | Contribution Sociale Généralisée | General Social Contribution | A social tax applied to most income types |
| CRDS | Contribution Remboursement Dette Sociale | Social Debt Repayment Contribution | Contribution to repay France's social debt |
| SECU | Sécurité Sociale | Social Security | Statutory health and social insurance contributions |
| RETRAITE | Retraite Complémentaire | Supplementary Pension | Additional pension contribution beyond the state minimum |
| SAL BRUT | Salaire Brut | Gross Salary | Total salary before deductions |
| SAL NET | Salaire Net | Net Salary | Your take-home pay after all deductions |

## Spanish Payroll Codes (ES)

| Code | Spanish Label | English Meaning | Plain Explanation |
|------|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| SS | Seguridad Social | Social Security | Contribution to Spain's social security system |
| IRPF | Impuesto Renta Personas Físicas | Personal Income Tax | Spanish personal income tax withheld at source |
| BRUTO | Salario Bruto | Gross Salary | Total earnings before deductions |
| NETO | Salario Neto | Net Salary | Your actual take-home pay |
| HORAS EXTRA | Horas Extraordinarias | Overtime Hours | Payment for hours worked beyond your standard schedule |

## General / Cross-Country Codes

| Code Pattern | Meaning | Plain Explanation |
|-------------|---------|-------------------|
| GROSS / BRUT | Gross Salary | Total earnings before any deductions |
| NET / NETO | Net Pay | Your take-home pay after all deductions |
| BONUS | Bonus | A one-time additional payment for performance or occasion |
| OVERTIME | Overtime | Extra pay for hours worked beyond the standard schedule |
| ALLOWANCE | Allowance | An additional payment for specific purposes (e.g., travel, meals) |
| TAX | Tax Withholding | Income tax deducted from your salary by your employer on behalf of the tax authority |
| PENSION | Pension Contribution | Your contribution towards your future retirement benefits |
| HEALTH INS | Health Insurance | Your contribution to health insurance coverage |
| DEDUCTION | Deduction | An amount subtracted from your gross pay |
